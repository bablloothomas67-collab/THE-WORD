THE WORD (വചനം)

Read. Understand. Reflect. Believe.

THE WORD is a Bible reading and Christian learning website in English and Malayalam. It represents Catholic, Orthodox and Protestant traditions fairly.

What you can do here, free

  • Read the Bible in English and Malayalam, with search, bookmarks, highlights and notes.
  • Daily verse and daily devotionals, with audio.
  • Reading plans and a private prayer journal.
  • Ask the Word, an AI Bible study assistant (5 questions a day).
  • Discover: Bible stories, Christian history, theology, apologetics and quizzes.
